# Fitliner organic growth plan

Technical SEO and useful on-site content create indexable assets; they do not create legitimate backlinks by themselves. Use this checklist after deployment and keep evidence of every completed action.

## Measurement and indexing

- [ ] Verify `https://www.befitliner.com` as a domain property in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- [ ] Submit `https://www.befitliner.com/sitemap.xml` and monitor discovered, crawled and indexed URLs by locale.
- [ ] Add `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` in production instead of hard-coding a verification token.
- [ ] Connect analytics that respects consent choices and record organic landing page, locale, app-store click and gym-lead conversion.
- [ ] Inspect the home, Health, gyms and each guide URL after deployment; request indexing only after canonical and hreflang validation.

## High-trust links worth earning

- [ ] Ask every participating gym to link from its official website to its public `gym-slug.befitliner.com` page with a factual label such as “Member access and gym information on Fitliner”.
- [ ] Add the same verified public gym URL to that gym’s Google Business Profile, social profiles and member onboarding material where appropriate.
- [ ] Ensure the Apple App Store and eventual Google Play listing use `https://www.befitliner.com` as the developer or marketing website.
- [ ] Publish an implementation case study with a consenting partner gym. Include original, aggregated evidence such as entry success rate, staff time saved or online-membership adoption; never expose member-level data.
- [ ] Offer useful technical commentary to fitness-industry publications about access migration, membership payments and outage planning. Link to the relevant guide only when it genuinely supports the article.
- [ ] Maintain accurate company and product profiles in reputable startup, fitness-technology and local-business directories. Avoid bulk directory services, paid link networks and reciprocal-link schemes.

## Content that can earn links

- [ ] Turn the gym-software checklist into a downloadable one-page procurement worksheet.
- [ ] Publish a transparent smart-access implementation checklist jointly with an installer or participating gym.
- [ ] Release a privacy-safe annual report using only sufficiently aggregated gym-traffic patterns, with methodology and limitations.
- [ ] Add real product release notes when a feature materially changes. Do not create thin posts for minor changes.
- [ ] Add named reviewers and review dates before expanding health-related content. Health pages require a higher trust standard than ordinary product copy.

## Editorial rules

- Create content because a member or gym operator can use it without visiting Google first.
- State who wrote or reviewed it, how it was prepared and why Fitliner is qualified to discuss the product behaviour.
- Separate measured facts, product behaviour, estimates and opinions.
- Never mass-produce city or keyword variants with substantially identical content.
- Do not buy followed links, automate outreach spam, publish fake reviews or create undisclosed competitor-comparison pages.

## Core KPIs

- Indexed canonical URLs and hreflang errors by locale.
- Non-branded impressions and qualified clicks to home, Health, gyms and guides.
- App-store outbound clicks and gym assessment starts from organic landing pages.
- Partner gym domains linking to their verified Fitliner page.
- Search snippets with correct title, description, favicon and brand name.
- Core Web Vitals for the 75th percentile of mobile visits.
